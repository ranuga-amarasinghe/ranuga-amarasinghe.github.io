01 / About

Curious by design.

My interests sit at the intersection of physical engineering and software — where code meets circuits, structures, sensors, and motion.

I’m especially interested in autonomous systems and robotics, from underwater vehicles and aerial platforms to small embedded projects. I like understanding how a system works as a whole: its mechanical design, electronics, control logic, and software.

Through team competitions and independent experimentation, I’ve explored building, testing, troubleshooting, and improving working prototypes. I’m continuing to develop my skills and document the projects I learn from.
