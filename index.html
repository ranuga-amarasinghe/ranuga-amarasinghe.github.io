01 / About

Curiosity meets engineering.

My interests lie at the intersection of robotics, mechanical systems, electronics and computing. I'm especially interested in machines that interact with the physical world, sense their surroundings and perform useful tasks.

Through practical projects, I explore engineering as an integrated discipline: understanding a problem, designing a system, developing its hardware and software, and learning through testing and iteration.

I'm continuing to develop my skills and document what I learn through projects, experiments and technical exploration.

Areas I'm exploring

  • Underwater and aerial robotics

  • Electronics, sensors and control
  • Programming and computer vision
  • Practical engineering problem-solving

Projects & experimentation.

A selection of my engineering experience and ongoing development. Project documentation and evidence will be added as they are prepared.

PROJECT 01

Underwater Robotics — SAUVC

Contributed as part of a team to an underwater robotics project associated with the Singapore Autonomous Underwater Vehicle Challenge (SAUVC). My involvement included work across mechanical design, electronics, software and computer vision. The system combined autonomous and remotely operated capabilities.

Underwater robotics Electronics Software Computer vision
PROJECT 02

WiFi Detective

A Flutter-based prototype exploring Wi-Fi signal measurements and their relationship to movement through an indoor space. The goal is to investigate signal mapping and walking-path visualization while accounting for the limitations of phone sensors and indoor positioning.

Flutter Wireless signals Data visualization Mobile development
