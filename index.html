About

Hello There!

I’m a student and maker from Sri Lanka with interests in robotics, embedded systems, mechanical design, and autonomous machines. I enjoy combining hardware and software to turn ideas into working projects.

I like understanding how different parts of a system fit together, from mechanical design and electronics to control systems and software. This has led me to explore everything from underwater robots and aerial platforms to smaller embedded projects.

Through team competitions and personal projects, I’ve gained hands-on experience in building, testing, troubleshooting, and improving prototypes. I’m always looking to learn new skills and document what I create. Outside engineering, I enjoy aerial photography and nature videography with my DJI Mini 4 Pro.
